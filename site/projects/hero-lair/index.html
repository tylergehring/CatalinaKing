Concept

Translating a story into architecture

The spaces reflect Simba's connection to family, his connection to nature, and his leadership.

The architecture was designed to blend with its surrounding environment.

This project is a home designed for Simba from The Lion King. The goal was to create a safe and peaceful place where Simba can live with his family while watching over the Pride Lands. The house is built into the side of a mountain so it blends into the natural landscape and overlooks the Serengeti savanna.
