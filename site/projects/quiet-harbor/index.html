Work / Hand Rendering

Quiet Harbor

Hand Renderings P1

One interior, four ways of seeing it. Guided by a written story about how someone would live in the room, Catalina rendered the same space in stippling, greyscale markers, graphite and pen, and let each technique change its atmosphere.

Path through the project

  1. 01

    Idea

    A narrative describing how someone experiences and uses the space.

  2. 02

    Iteration

    Four renderings: stippling, greyscale markers, graphite and pen.

  3. 03

    Final

    Four iterations of the same space.

Concept

Narrative first

Before creating the renderings, Catalina developed a narrative describing how she imagined someone experiencing and using the space. The narrative then guided each rendering.

The project demonstrates material representation, texture, value, atmosphere, hand rendering and visual storytelling.
