Olivier's Chocolate

A Taste of Belgium, brought to Moscow, Idaho

A taste of Belgium in Moscow, Idaho. Starting from a class-wide urban renewal study at 6th and Jackson, Catalina designed a chocolate shop for a fictional Belgian chocolatier, with two homes above it, drawing on Belgian architecture, materials and craftsmanship.

Concept

A new home, a new community

The concept follows a fictional Belgian chocolatier named Olivier, who moves to Moscow, Idaho to share his craft and build a new community.

The design draws from Belgian architecture, materials and craftsmanship, and explores identity, home, community and cultural connection.

The building includes a chocolate shop, two private living spaces with bedrooms, bathrooms and compact kitchens, and a shared washer/dryer.
