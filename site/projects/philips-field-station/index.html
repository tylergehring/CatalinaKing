Work / Research Through Design

Philips Field Station

Project 2 — Research Through Design

Architecture as a bridge between people and landscape. Inspired by the system of pollination, this field station supports two student researchers living and working at Philips Farm, with room for observation, study and collaboration, and a way for visitors to experience the site's ecology.

Concept

Architecture as a bridge

The architectural concept treats architecture as a bridge between people and landscape.

This research center is inspired by the complex system of pollination and the interconnected relationships that sustain the ecology of Philips Farm in Moscow, Idaho. The project explores how plants, pollinators, researchers, and visitors are all connected within the landscape, creating a space that celebrates nature through design while remaining sensitive and responsible to the local environment.
